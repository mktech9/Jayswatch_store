Born from a Passion for Mechanical Timekeeping

Chronoswiss was founded in Munich, Germany, in 1983 by experienced watchmaker Gerd-Rüdiger Lang. At a time when the world was embracing quartz technology, Lang remained devoted to the art of traditional mechanical watchmaking. His vision was clear to preserve the craftsmanship, character, and mechanical complexity of classic timepieces in a modern era.

From its earliest days, Chronoswiss stood apart by focusing solely on mechanical watches. The brand’s commitment to hand-finished movements and distinctive designs made it a favorite among watch enthusiasts who valued precision engineering and timeless style. Every Chronoswiss watch shows the beauty of mechanical craftsmanship and the talent of its makers.

Mastering the Art of Detail and Design

What makes Chronoswiss truly unique is its dedication to fine details and unmistakable designs. The brand introduced several signature features that quickly became its identity including the onion-shaped crown, knurled bezel, and skeletonized dials that beautifully showcase the watch’s inner workings.

In 1987, Chronoswiss introduced the Regulateur, a watch that separated the hours, minutes, and seconds into different subdials, offering a clear and classic display inspired by historical regulator clocks. This iconic model remains one of the brand’s most recognized creations and continues to evolve through modern interpretations.

Chronoswiss also pioneered transparent case backs long before it became a common feature in luxury watches, allowing wearers to admire the intricate mechanical movements within. Every timepiece is assembled and decorated by hand, combining traditional Swiss techniques with bold, contemporary styling.

Carrying Swiss Watchmaking into a New Era

Though founded in Germany, Chronoswiss later moved its headquarters to Lucerne, Switzerland, placing itself at the heart of the world’s luxury watchmaking capital. The brand continues to innovate while preserving its independent spirit and commitment to mechanical excellence.

Modern collections like the Open Gear ReSec and Flying Regulator showcase Chronoswiss’s ability to blend avant-garde design with classical elements. With bold colors, 3D dials, and open-worked movements, these timepieces appeal to a new generation of watch collectors while staying true to the brand’s origins.

Today, Chronoswiss stands out for its unique mix of tradition, innovation, and expert watchmaking. Each watch reflects the brand’s deep love for watchmaking, created for those who value the lasting beauty of mechanical craftsmanship.
