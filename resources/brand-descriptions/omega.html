A Journey Rooted in Precision and Innovation

The story of Omega began in a modest Swiss workshop in 1848, where visionary watchmaker Louis Brandt set out to create timepieces that married elegant design with impeccable mechanical precision. What started as a small, family-run operation quickly evolved into one of the most celebrated names in the world of fine watchmaking. The defining moment came with the creation of the groundbreaking “Omega” caliber — a movement so reliable and advanced for its time that the brand adopted its name, symbolizing ultimate achievement in horology.

From that day forward, Omega has stood for innovation, performance, and the relentless pursuit of excellence. Known for designing timepieces capable of withstanding the most extreme conditions — whether on land, beneath the ocean, or in the vastness of space — Omega established itself as a trusted partner for pioneers and adventurers worldwide.

Trusted by Explorers, Athletes, Icons, and Astronauts

Unlike many luxury brands, Omega has always been actively involved in some of history’s greatest moments. The brand has proudly served as the official timekeeper of the Olympic Games since 1932, ensuring the accuracy of every race, jump, and finish that defines athletic greatness.

In the realm of space exploration, Omega achieved an unparalleled milestone. In 1969, when Buzz Aldrin stepped onto the lunar surface, he was wearing the Omega Speedmaster Professional, famously dubbed the “Moonwatch.” It remains the only watch flight-qualified by NASA for all manned space missions.

Omega’s reputation extends underwater too. Its Seamaster collection became a favorite of professional divers and adventure seekers, and its presence in popular culture was cemented when James Bond, first played by Pierce Brosnan in the 1995 film GoldenEye, sported a Seamaster — a tradition proudly continued by Daniel Craig in subsequent Bond films.

Omega proudly collaborates with a remarkable group of brand ambassadors and global icons, each embodying the brand’s bold and forward-thinking spirit in a lineup that reflects its spirit of bold achievement and contemporary sophistication. Among them are acclaimed actor George Clooney, award-winning actress Nicole Kidman, Academy Award winner Eddie Redmayne, supermodel Cindy Crawford, and her daughter Kaia Gerber, representing the next generation of style icons.

The brand’s dynamic circle also includes Daniel Craig, who famously wore the Omega Seamaster as James Bond, tennis legend Roger Federer, Olympic champion Michael Phelps, golf star Rory McIlroy, and South Korean actor Hyun Bin.

These global personalities embody Omega’s values of innovation, performance, and modern distinction — making them the perfect representatives of the brand’s forward-thinking legacy in luxury watchmaking.

Timeless Craftsmanship and Technological Excellence

Each Omega timepiece is a blend of sophisticated design and cutting-edge technology. Meticulously crafted in Switzerland, every watch is subjected to the highest standards of testing for accuracy, durability, and aesthetics. Omega has consistently led the way in watchmaking innovation. One of its most notable breakthroughs is the Co-Axial escapement movement, designed to reduce friction, improve accuracy, and extend service intervals compared to traditional mechanisms.

The brand also tackled a major challenge in modern horology: magnetism. Omega developed movements resistant to magnetic fields of up to 15,000 gauss, protecting the watch’s precision even around everyday electronic devices.

To guarantee top-tier performance, Omega introduced Master Chronometer certification, awarded by METAS. This ensures each timepiece meets strict standards for accuracy, durability, water resistance, and anti-magnetic protection, reaffirming Omega’s status as a leader in both innovation and reliability.

Whether you're captivated by the space-tested Speedmaster, the ocean-ready Seamaster, or the refined elegance of the Constellation and De Ville collections, Omega watches are crafted to accompany life’s boldest adventures and finest moments — offering a timepiece that’s not just worn, but lived in.
