Est. 1955 — La Chaux-de-Fonds

A Bold Beginning in
Swiss Watchmaking

The story of Corum began in 1955, when watchmakers René Bannwart and his uncle Gaston Ries founded the brand in La Chaux-de-Fonds, Switzerland, one of the world’s most famous watchmaking capitals. Right from the start, Corum set out to break traditional boundaries and challenge conventional designs with bold, imaginative timepieces.

Within just a few years, the brand made its mark with distinctive creations that combined technical precision with artistic flair. Corum quickly became known for pushing the limits of design while maintaining the highest standards of Swiss watchmaking craftsmanship. Even today, that daring spirit remains at the heart of the brand’s identity.

Crafting Icons:
From Coin Watches to Admiral’s Cup

Corum's signature lies in its creativity and originality, breaking the mold with timepieces that seamlessly blend technical precision with extraordinary, unconventional design.

The Coin Watch

One of its first big achievements in 1964. This unique timepiece featured an ultra-thin movement housed inside an actual gold coin, including the famous U.S. Double Eagles and $20 Liberty coins. It became an instant collector’s piece and a symbol of the brand’s innovative character.

Admiral’s Cup

Debuting in 1960, this nautical-themed line is distinguished by its bold 12-sided bezel and colorful maritime flags. It soon became a favorite among both sailing fans and people who enjoy luxury watches. Over the decades, the Admiral’s Cup has evolved in style and materials but continues to embody Corum’s adventurous, sporty elegance.

Concept Editions

Corum gained fame for creating daring limited editions and concept watches, often blending precious metals with bold, modern aesthetics and unique complications—a powerful combination that defines the brand today.

Modern Artistry

Innovation with a
Contemporary Edge

In the modern era, Corum continues to uphold its reputation for innovation while staying true to its rich heritage. Collections like the Bubble series, known for its oversized domed sapphire crystal and playful, artistic dials, offer a fresh, unconventional take on luxury timepieces. These bold designs have attracted a new generation of collectors who appreciate creativity alongside craftsmanship.

Despite changing trends in the watch industry, Corum remains one of the few independent Swiss brands that dares to blend tradition with modern artistry. Every Corum watch tells a story of bold ideas, precise mechanics, and a fearless approach to design — making it a true statement piece for watch enthusiasts around the world.
