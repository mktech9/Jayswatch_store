A Legacy Born in the Heart of Rome

A long time ago, in 1884, a talented Greek silversmith named Sotirios Voulgaris moved to the beautiful city of Rome. Captivated by Rome’s ancient ruins, grand monuments, and timeless charm, he decided to start a small jewelry shop. This humble beginning marked the birth of what we now know as Bvlgari.

As the years passed, Bvlgari became much more than a local jeweler. It grew into one of Italy’s most famous luxury brands, admired for its bold, eye-catching designs. The brand had a special way of blending the beauty of ancient art with a modern, fashionable touch.

Jewelry had always been Bvlgari’s main passion, but the mid-1900s brought a thrilling new direction for the brand stepped into the world of fine watches. Combining Italy’s sense of style with Swiss watchmaking expertise, Bvlgari created timepieces that weren’t just about keeping time — they were also beautiful works of art, meant to be worn with pride and admired for their elegance and precision.

Designing Time: The Bvlgari Signature Look

As time went on, Bvlgari continued to make its mark on the world of luxury watches. The brand didn’t just follow trends it created them. Inspired by the rich history of Rome and the effortless style of Italian culture, Bvlgari introduced watch designs that felt fresh, bold, and different from anything people had seen before.

In 1977, something remarkable happened. Bvlgari launched the Bvlgari watch, a timepiece that quickly became a worldwide sensation. Its standout feature was a double-logo engraved bezel, a design inspired by the inscriptions found on ancient Roman monuments. With its clean, minimalist look and daring character, this watch changed the way people thought about luxury timepieces and set new standards in watch design.

To take its watchmaking skills to the next level, Bvlgari turned to the heart of precision craftsmanship Switzerland. The brand invested in top-tier Swiss workshops, known as ateliers, where expert watchmakers brought their skills to every tiny detail, from intricate movements to flawless finishes. This beautiful combination of Italian creativity and Swiss expertise became the signature of Bvlgari watches. It led to the creation of unforgettable collections like the Serpenti, known for its snake-like elegance, and the Octo Finissimo, which earned worldwide praise for its record-breaking, ultra-slim design.

Modern Icons for the Next Generation

In recent years, Bvlgari has proven itself as a true innovator in modern horology. The Octo Finissimo series has set several world records for thinness, offering ultra-slim, architectural designs paired with high-performance mechanical movements. These watches are admired by collectors and style leaders for their bold aesthetics and groundbreaking engineering.

Bvlgari continues to blend history with innovation, honoring its Roman roots while constantly pushing the boundaries of design and technology. Every timepiece carries the brand’s signature elegance and daring character, making it more than just a watch — it’s a wearable piece of Roman-inspired art, crafted for those who appreciate luxury with personality and history.
