The Story of Rolex

A Legacy of Excellence

For over a century, Rolex has represented excellence, innovation, and precision in the world of luxury watchmaking. The journey began in 1905 in London when Hans Wilsdorf and Alfred Davis established a company called Wilsdorf & Davis.

In 1908, Wilsdorf introduced the name “Rolex,” choosing it for its simplicity, ease of pronunciation across languages, and suitability for watch dials. By 1920, the company had relocated to Geneva, Switzerland, where it officially became known as Montres Rolex S.A., laying the foundation for its enduring reputation in Swiss horology.
