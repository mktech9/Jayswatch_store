Tudor: A Brand Born from a Legacy

Hans Wilsdorf, the founder of Rolex, introduced Tudor in 1926 with the aim of providing durable and precise watches that maintained Rolex’s standards while being more budget-friendly. Created as a sister brand to Rolex, Tudor quickly built a reputation for crafting reliable, stylish, and high-performance watches that didn’t compromise on quality.

Over the years, Tudor evolved into a respected name in its own right, known for producing robust tool watches designed for professional use — from diving and motorsports to military operations. Though connected to Rolex in its early years, Tudor developed its own identity, offering distinctive designs and innovative features that appealed to a new generation of watch enthusiasts.

Timepieces Built for Adventure

Tudor watches have always stood out for their strength, accuracy, and bold style. The brand made history with its Oyster Prince collection in the 1950s, famously worn by the Royal Navy and various military forces around the world. These watches earned a reputation for reliability in the harshest conditions, from icy expeditions to deep-sea dives.

Another iconic line is the Tudor Submariner, introduced in 1954, which became a trusted tool for professional divers and naval officers. Built for reliability and known for its clean, functional look, the Submariner played a key role in establishing Tudor’s standing in the diving watch category.

In recent times, the Black Bay collection from Tudor has earned a special place among watch enthusiasts and collectors. Inspired by the brand’s rich heritage, these watches feature vintage-style elements like snowflake hands and domed crystals, combined with modern technology and in-house movements.

A Perfect Balance of Heritage and Modern Performance

Today, Tudor watches are admired for offering the perfect blend of classic style and contemporary performance. Manufactured in Switzerland with exceptional attention to detail, each timepiece undergoes rigorous testing to meet the brand’s high standards of durability and accuracy.

Tudor has earned attention for partnering with well-known ambassadors like global football star David Beckham and New Zealand’s legendary All Blacks rugby team, both symbolizing the brand’s commitment to adventure and bold design.

Whether you prefer the classic style of the Black Bay, the bold and sporty look of the Pelagos, or the refined elegance of the Royal collection, Tudor creates timepieces for those who value craftsmanship, heritage, and contemporary design in a single watch.
