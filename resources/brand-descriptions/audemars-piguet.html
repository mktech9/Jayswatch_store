Why Choose Audemars Piguet? A Legacy of Craftsmanship

In the tranquil Swiss village of Vallée de Joux, Jules Louis Audemars and Edward Auguste Piguet founded Audemars Piguet in 1875, driven by a vision to craft exceptional watches that blend precise mechanics with daring design.

The world took notice in 1972 when the Royal Oak was introduced — a game-changing stainless steel watch with a distinctive octagonal bezel and exposed screws. This was more than a watch; it was a revolution. Today, every pre-owned Audemars Piguet watch continues this spirit of fearless innovation and impeccable craftsmanship.
